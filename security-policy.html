Security Policy for Hertz Logic Machinery India LLP

Last Updated: 2026-06-22

Our Commitment to Security

At Hertz Logic Machinery India LLP, we consider the security of our systems a top priority. However, no matter how much effort we put into system security, there can still be vulnerabilities present.

If you discover a vulnerability, we would like to know about it so we can take steps to address it as quickly as possible. We would like to ask you to help us better protect our clients and our systems.

How to Report a Security Vulnerability

If you have discovered a security vulnerability, please report it to us by emailing security@example.com.

Please include the following details in your report:

  • A detailed description of the vulnerability, including the steps to reproduce it.
  • The URL or IP address of the affected system.
  • Any proof-of-concept code, screenshots, or screen recordings to help us understand the issue.
  • Your name and contact information (optional, can be anonymous).
PGP Encryption: For sensitive information, we strongly encourage you to encrypt your report using our PGP key, which can be found at: https://example.com/pgp-key.asc.

Scope

This policy applies to the following systems and services:

  • *.example.com

Out of Scope

The following are considered out of scope:

  • Denial of Service (DoS or DDoS) attacks.
  • Social engineering or phishing of our staff or customers.
  • Physical attacks against our employees, offices, or data centers.
  • Reports from automated scanners without a proof-of-concept.
  • Missing security headers that do not lead to a direct vulnerability.
  • Self-XSS and issues exploitable only through Self-XSS.

What to Expect (Our Process)

After you submit a report, we will:

  1. Send an automated acknowledgment of your report within 24 hours.
  2. Provide a non-automated response within 3 business days to confirm the vulnerability and begin our investigation.
  3. Keep you updated on our progress and notify you when the vulnerability has been fixed.
  4. With your permission, we will publicly acknowledge your contribution on our Hall of Fame page.

Safe Harbor

We consider security research and vulnerability disclosure activities conducted under this policy to be authorized. We will not pursue civil or criminal action, or initiate a complaint to law enforcement, against researchers for accidental, good-faith violations of this policy.

To be protected by this Safe Harbor provision, you must:

  • Comply with all guidelines in this policy.
  • Avoid privacy violations, destruction of data, and interruption or degradation of our services.
  • Not access or modify data that does not belong to you.

Acknowledgments (Hall of Fame)

We value the contributions of security researchers who help us maintain a secure environment. We offer our gratitude and public recognition to them on our Hall of Fame page.
