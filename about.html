About Hertz Logic Machinery India LLP

Your trusted partner for premium beverage equipment spare parts

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Hertz Logic Machinery India LLP is a professional spare parts supplier in the beverage industry. Our main products include spare parts for Krones and KHS machines. Our engineers have more than 15 years of industry experience.

Our advantage is high quality, fast delivery, competitive price, and providing a one-stop service for all your beverage equipment needs.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, reliable beverage equipment spare parts at competitive prices, ensuring our customers can keep their operations running smoothly and efficiently. We believe in building long-term partnerships through exceptional service and product excellence.
